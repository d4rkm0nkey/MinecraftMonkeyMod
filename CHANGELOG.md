# Cheeky Monkeys 1.0.0-beta.1

The first release for Minecraft 26.3, rebuilt from scratch for Fabric and NeoForge. This is a beta: expect rough edges, and please report bugs on GitHub.

**Monkeys**
- Troops of monkeys live in jungles, and grey, red-faced snow monkeys in snowy taigas and groves
- Seventeen animations: walking, galloping, sitting, sleeping, dancing to jukeboxes, pounding their chests and more
- They climb trunks, swing hand over hand under the canopy, sit on top of the trees and leap from tree to tree and over gaps

**Mischief**
- Monkeys race for bananas you throw and run off to eat them
- Wild monkeys sneak up behind you and pick your pockets, then hide the loot in stash nests up in the trees. Hit the thief, offer it a banana or find its nest to get your things back
- Hurt one and the whole troop screeches and scatters up the trees

**Pets**
- Tame monkeys with bananas, then switch between follow, stay, wander and gather
- Pets fetch items lying around, snatch weapons out of monsters' hands and warn you about monsters coming for you
- Sneak and right-click to carry your monkey on your shoulder

**Also**
- Banana bunches grow on jungle trees
- Six new advancements
- `config/cheeky_monkeys.json` turns pickpocketing, stash nests and the pet abilities on or off

Requires GeckoLib 5.5.7+ (and Fabric API on Fabric).
